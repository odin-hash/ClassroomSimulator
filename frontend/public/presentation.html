The Classroom Challenge
02 / 07

The "Theoretical" B.Ed Gap

Traditional teacher training lacks real behavioral complexity and adaptive language preparation.

Low-Stakes Mock Teaching

Trainees practice speaking to empty rooms or peer students who act cooperatively. It completely fails to mimic the dynamic chaos of an active Indian classroom.

Unprepared for Misbehavior

Trainees get blindsided by natural classroom disruptions: whispering, attention drop, active interruption, and student confusion.

The Accent & Code-Switching Barrier

Classrooms in diverse regions require teachers to communicate fluently in mixtures of English, Hindi, and Bengali (Hinglish/Benglish). Without interactive training in local dialects, explanation delivery remains stiff and scripted.

The Innovation
03 / 07

The Immersive Sandbox Solution

A real-time behavioral training environment equipped with dynamic orchestration tools.

Persistent Persona Agents

6 persistent virtual students—Aarav, Ananya, Kabir, Ishaan, Riya, and Vihaan—each possessing persistent psychological parameters (curiosity, confidence, active attention) and custom speech engines.

Accented Code-Switching Voice Input

Trainees lead sessions using their natural teaching accents in English, Hindi, or Bengali. Dynamic STT accurately transcribes code-switched mixtures into native scripts.

Interactive Classroom Controls

A multi-mode sandbox workspace letting trainees direct custom lessons, sketch presets on the interactive whiteboard, warning disruptive students, and simple explanations to re-engage shy pupils in real-time.

Live Behavioral Events

Simulates random, high-fidelity occurrences (e.g., student zoning out or asking highly challenging curricular questions) to test classroom reflex skills.

Technical Pipeline

The Real-Time AI Pipeline

Under the hood: A low-latency feedback loop powering classroom state and speech synthesis.

🎤
Teacher Input
Voice / Text in native accent routed to dynamic STT engine.
📉
State Engine
Applies attention decays, confusion levels, and triggers random events.
🎯
Persona Router
Determines which of the 6 students responds based on personality profiles.
🧠
Gemini 2.0
Generates raw JSON response containing emotional delta, response, and memory updates.
🔊
Edge Neural TTS
Streams student's voice using dynamic pitch shifting per student.

Presentation Blueprint
07 / 07

Delivering a Wow-Factor Demo

Use this optimized 3-minute sequence during a live panel presentation to showcase all core features.

0:00 - 0:45
Initialization: Open the dashboard. Select हिंदी (Hindi) or বাংলা (Bengali) class language to instantly capture the panel's attention. Click **Start Session**.
0:45 - 1:30
Microphone & STT Test: Click the microphone. Speak a natural sentence in Hindi/Bengali (e.g. asking about gravity). Point out the flawless Devanagari/Bengali transcription in the text bar.
1:30 - 2:15
Neural Student Reply: Let the student respond. Highlight the natural, child-like neural voice of the responding student card and their synchronized emotion indicator delta.
2:15 - 3:00
Event Resolution & Scorecard: Wait for a random behavioral event (e.g. confusion). Click the student's card and apply a **Teacher Action** to resolve it. Then end the session and present the B.Ed scorecard!
